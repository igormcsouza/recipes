title: Missionary Fricassee
description: A Brazilian creamy chicken bake with corn and melted cheese. Easy, serves 6.
lead: Creamy shredded chicken, corn and melted cheese on top. An easy Brazilian chicken bake that serves 6 (or 2 hungry Elders!).
ingredients:
  - text: 1 kg cooked, shredded chicken
  - text: 1 can sweet corn
  - text: 1 tub requeijão
    note: Substitute with cream cheese.
  - text: 1 box creme de leite (about 200 g)
    note: Brazilian table cream. Substitute with table cream or light cream.
  - text: "Seasoning: sweet peppers (pimenta de cheiro), onion, garlic, dried herbs and salt"
  - text: 150 g sliced cheese
  - text: Shoestring potato sticks (batata palha)
    note: Crispy fried potato topping, sold in Brazilian shops.
steps:
  - Pressure-cook the chicken with the seasoning for 40 min. Shred it and keep the stock and the cooked seasoning.
  - "Blend the cream, requeijão, corn, strained cooked seasoning and 1/2 cup of the stock. Make 1 liter of blend at most, so the dish does not get watery."
  - Mix the shredded chicken with the sauce in a baking dish.
  - In a preheated oven (15 min), cover with cheese and bake until golden, 10-15 min.
  - Finish with the potato sticks and serve hot!
